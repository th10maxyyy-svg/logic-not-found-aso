OFFICIAL PRIVACY POLICY

Logic Not Found — Privacy Policy

Developer / Publisher: Dev Bhai Productions
App Package ID: com.devbhai.logicnotfound
Effective Date: November 2026

1. Overview

Logic Not Found is an offline-capable comedic puzzle and brain-teaser game developed by Dev Bhai Productions. We respect your privacy and designed the game so you can play without creating an account or providing personal identity information.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

  • Game Progress & Preferences: Your cleared levels, stars, virtual Aura (✨), unlocked skins, custom levels, and audio/accessibility settings are stored locally on your device using standard Unity PlayerPrefs.
  • Clipboard (Optional User Action): When you explicitly tap buttons such as Copy Save Code, Share Report Card, or Copy Custom Level Code, the game copies or reads that text string from your device clipboard solely to perform your requested action. It is never transmitted to any server.

3. Third-Party Advertising & In-App Purchases

When published with advertising and store billing enabled, the app may use trusted third-party SDKs:

  • Advertising (e.g., Google AdMob / Unity Ads / AppLovin): Ad networks may use device advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID or Apple IDFA, subject to your OS permission), coarse IP location, and ad interaction diagnostics to display Banner, Interstitial, and Rewarded ads.
  • In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing / Apple StoreKit): All financial transactions (such as Remove Forced Ads or VIP Packs) are processed securely and directly by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never receive or store your credit card or payment details.

4. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. All in-game items (Aura ✨, skins, and brain-rot creatures) are 100% virtual cartoon items with zero real-money gambling or cash-out value.

5. Your Choices & Data Deletion

You can delete 100% of your local game data at any time inside the game by opening ⚙️ Settings → 🗑️ Reset Save, or by uninstalling the application from your device.
